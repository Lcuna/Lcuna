I am an Applied Biology graduate with experience in molecular diagnostics, health research and data analysis. My path connects the laboratory, the field and data: from HIV and SARS-CoV-2 diagnostics and genetic sequencing, to managing data for a study on the prevention of mother-to-child transmission of HIV, to leading monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEAL) in community development projects.

I use this space to share the code behind my data work and to document what I am learning.

---

### 🔬 Research interests

- Biostatistics and epidemiology applied to **HIV, malaria and tuberculosis**
- Bioinformatics and **genomic surveillance** of pathogens
- Health indicators and health equity in **African countries**

### 🌱 Currently

- Completing a Postgraduate Programme in Scientific Research in Biomedicine (University of Cape Verde and University of Lisbon)
- Strengthening my skills in **R** and **Python** for statistical analysis and reproducible research
- Learning the basics of NGS data analysis
